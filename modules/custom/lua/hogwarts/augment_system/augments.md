# Custom Augment System

This page explains how to add special powers, called **augments**, to your gear.
Think of an augment like a sticker that makes your equipment stronger. You bring Cecil Harvey one piece of gear and the right materials, and he adds the powers for you.

## Where to go

Talk to **Cecil Harvey** in **Southern San d'Oria**.  You will find him near the minstrels that play along the wall that separates Northern and Southern San d'Oria.

## The short version

1. Pick **one piece of equipment** you want to improve.
2. Pick up to **four different augment materials** from the tables below.
3. Trade the equipment and the materials to Cecil Harvey.
4. Cecil tells you what your item will get.
5. If the preview looks right, trade the **same equipment and same materials again**.
6. Cecil keeps the traded items, works on the gear, and gives back the augmented equipment.

> Important: The second trade is your confirmation. Only trade the same items again if you are sure.

## Easy rules to remember

- You may augment **one equipment item at a time**.
- You may add up to **four different augments** at once.
- You cannot add the **same augment twice** on one item.
- If your gear is a higher tier, it can use augments from its own tier and lower tiers that you have unlocked.
- If a material does not match an augment Cecil can use, he will not accept it for augmenting.
- If you only trade one equipment item, Cecil gives you a hint about the item's tier instead of augmenting it.

## Special Notes

- Augments are only active on equipment while your level is equal to or greater than the level required to equip that item.
-- Example: An augmented Shade Harness requires level 25 for those augments to be active.
-- Example: Wearing that Shade Harness in a level 20 capped battlefield will temporarily deactivate the augments.
-- Example: Leaving the capped area will restore the augments on the Shade Harness.
- Summary: In level capped areas, augment and wear items equal to or less than the level cap.
- Some stats have known maximum values in-game, and provide no additional benefit beyond this cap.  Check the table below.
- Blending offensive and defensive augments is generally useful for adventurous play.
- Maximizing values for effectiveness as your given role is typically recommended for challenging content:
-- Tanks: accuracy, enmity generation, damage reduction, -enemy crit rate, VIT, fast cast, refresh, haste
-- Melee: accuracy, weapon damage, weapon delay, haste, attack, str, dex, crit rate, crit damage
-- Ranged: accuracy, weapon damage, weapon delay, snapshot, attack, str, agi, crit rate, crit damage
-- Magic: +magic accuracy, +magic attack, +magic damage, elemental affinity, fast cast, haste, refresh
-- Pet: +pet statistics that match role (tank, melee, ranged, magic)
-- Healer: +cure potency, +regen, fast cast, refresh, mnd, haste
-- Support: fast cast, +refresh, +enhancing duration, +regen, mnd

Cecil checks special key items to decide which augment tiers you may use. Tier 0 is the starting tier. Higher tiers need these key items, in order:

| Tier | Key item needed | Source |
|---:|---|---|

| 1 | Limit Breaker | Achieve level 75.  Talk to the nomad moogle near Maat. |

| 3 | Airship Pass | Achieve Rank 5 for your starting nation. |
| 4 | Rhapsody in Umber | Complete Rhapsodies of Vana Diel missions. |

## How the trade and confirmation works

### First trade: preview

Trade Cecil:

- The equipment you want to augment.
- The material stack or stacks for the augments you want.

Cecil will look at the trade and show a preview. The preview tells you:

- The tier of your equipment.
- The augment effects that will be added.

Nothing is taken during the preview. This is Cecil saying, "Here is what will happen. Are you sure?"

### Second trade: confirmation

If the preview is correct, trade the **exact same equipment and materials** again.

This time, Cecil treats the trade as your "yes." He will:

1. Take the equipment and materials.
2. Do some things only he understands.
3. Give you back the same equipment with the new augments on it.

If you change the item or materials, Cecil will show a new preview instead of finishing the augment.
